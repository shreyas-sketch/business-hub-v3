    Six engines. One Business Brain.

    Every business runs on the same six engines. OwnerDesk gives each one its own tools, and they all work from one Business Brain, so you describe your business once.

    Up and running in an afternoon.

    You don't set up six tools. You tell OwnerDesk about your business once, and it shows you where to start.

    1. Fill in your Knowledge Hub

      From your website, a voice note or a few typed lines: what you sell, your prices, your customers and your targets. Every page and every AI task uses it.

    2. Take the Engine Audit

      15 questions across the six engines. You see what each gap costs you in hours and rupees, and what fixing it unlocks.

    3. Switch on what matters first

      Start with the engine that costs you the most. The rest are waiting on the same desk, already filled in from your Knowledge Hub.

      Money waits for you

      Payment reminders and quotations are written for you and wait for your tap. Prices and quotes never go out on their own.

      New customers hear from you

      OwnerDesk finds businesses that fit and drafts the first message. You send each one from your own WhatsApp or email.

      Replies come from your number

      Customers get answers from your own WhatsApp, so the conversation stays yours. Your team sees every chat.

      Every number is counted

      What the AI did today and this month is counted from your own records. Hours saved is the only estimate, and it says so.

      Call recordings aren't kept

      The call coach writes out the call, scores it and deletes the draft within 7 days. The recording itself is never stored.

      What your key person knows is written down as SOPs and a role card, so the business doesn't stop when one person is away.
